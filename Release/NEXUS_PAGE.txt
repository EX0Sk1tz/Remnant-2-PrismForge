Mod title:
Prismforge - Live Prism & Stat Editor for Remnant II

Summary line (short description field):
Edit prisms and character attributes in Remnant II while the game runs. Change segment stats and levels, add extra segments, hold any stat past its normal limit. Steam and Game Pass.

--- Description (paste into the description editor, BBCode mode) ---

[size=5][b]Prismforge[/b][/size]
[b]Live prism & stat editor for Remnant II[/b]

Edit your [b]prisms[/b] and [b]character attributes[/b] while Remnant II is running. Change a value, press Apply, and it is live in game. No save-file swapping.

[b][color=#e07b3c]Tested on the Steam and Game Pass versions.[/color][/b]

[size=4][b]New in 2.4.0[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Prism presets[/b]: save prism layouts under a name ("Mod focus", "Ranged focus") and load one into any prism with one click, on every character. Undo puts the old layout back
[/list]

[size=4][b]New in 2.1.0[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Add segment any time[/b]: a full prism gets more room from the game's own memory allocator, no workaround needed
[*][b]Remove or reorder any segment[/b], and remove fed fragments
[*][b]Reset prism[/b]: turn a prism back into a blank one and build it up again from level 0
[/list]

[size=4][b]New in 2.0.0[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Segment levels up to 100,000,000[/b]: fusion bonuses keep scaling with level, standard segments stop at level 10
[*][b]Game Pass / Microsoft Store version supported[/b]
[*][b]Support zip[/b] for bug reports, safer attribute writes
[/list]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]All prisms on your character[/b] with name, level, segments and fed fragments
[*][b]Change any segment[/b]: 45 standard stats, 23 fusions, 42 legendaries, with in-game names and descriptions
[*][b]Change segment levels and pending XP[/b]; fusion bonuses scale with level
[*][b]Extra segments[/b] beyond the usual six, remove and reorder segments, reset a prism (experimental)
[*][b]Presets and build codes[/b]: save layouts and switch a prism between them in one click, share them as codes
[*]Edits are staged and only written on [b]Apply[/b]; every write is read back and confirmed
[*][b]Discard[/b] and [b]Revert to session start[/b] to undo
[*][b]Attributes[/b]: live list of ~220 character stats (damage, crit, weak spot, speeds, resistances, caps)
[*][b]Hold[/b] any stat at your value, even when the game recalculates it
[*][b]Soft warnings[/b] for risky values (above a game cap, chances over 100 %, reductions at 100 %)
[*]Connects to the game by itself, no admin rights, single self-contained exe
[/list]

[b]Note:[/b] above level 10 only fusion bonuses keep growing; standard segments stay at their level-10 value. Re-equip the prism after Apply, the game applies bonuses on equip.

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*][b]Back up your saves[/b]: copy [i]%USERPROFILE%\Saved Games\Remnant2[/i] somewhere safe
[*]Unzip anywhere
[*]Start Remnant II and load a character
[*]Run [b]Prismforge.exe[/b]
[/list]
Uninstall: delete the exe. Settings and logs are in [i]%LocalAppData%\Prismforge[/i].

[size=4][b]Please read[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Edits end up in your save at the next autosave. [b]Back up first.[/b]
[*]Unusual values can crash the game. Start moderate.
[*]Held attributes stay active after closing the editor until you press [b]Release all[/b] or restart the game.
[*]Changed legendary segments take full effect after reloading your character.
[*]In co-op, extreme stats affect other players too.
[*]If the Cheat Engine table's "System Statistics" script is active, disable it before using Hold.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Antivirus[/b][/size]
The editor reads and writes the game's memory and, for Hold, patches one instruction in the running game. Some antivirus tools flag every program that does this. Source code: [url=https://github.com/EX0Sk1tz/Remnant-2-PrismForge]GitHub[/url].

[size=4][b]Known limitations[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Fed-fragment editing untested
[*]Extreme fusion levels can break the game (e.g. cooldowns below zero) and are saved with your character
[*]Replacing an action-based legendary (e.g. Unbreakable) may keep the old effect until reload
[/list]

[size=4][b]Bug reports[/b][/size]
Press [b]Support zip[/b] in the editor's bottom bar and attach the zip, plus what you did.
